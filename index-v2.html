What we believe

Better products and services are how European companies win, and understanding customers is where better products start. Every call, chat and email already holds that understanding. A company that reads all of it makes better decisions than one that still guesses from a sample.

Before you ask

Our CRM, NPS and speech analytics already tell us this

CRM records what customers did. Surveys record what the 5% who answer chose to say. Speech analytics spots keywords. None of them reads a conversation for meaning and answers a business question with evidence.

Contact-centre data is biased towards people with problems

It is. It is also the largest unfiltered account of what your customers think, and it includes the ones you are about to lose. We report the base, segment every finding, and read it alongside your survey.

What do we need to give you?

For a Pulse: one export of recordings or transcripts, chats and emails, into your own cloud. Read-only. Nothing changes for your agents or systems.

Is our data safe, and is this allowed under GDPR?

Everything runs inside your own European environment. Conversations never leave your perimeter, every finding is traceable to its source, and retention stays under your control. We can walk your DPO through it.

Which languages?

Any your customers use. The largest deployment runs on millions of calls in Taglish, a mix of Tagalog and English, scored on one standard.
