What it supervises

Your playbook, applied to every conversation

AI Quality Assurance scores each conversation against your own sales or support process — across 100% of interactions, not a random sample.

  • Agents follow the defined sales or support process
  • Chat-support quality supervised alongside calls
  • Sales-conversation quality scored across calls and chat
  • Performance monitored across teams and individual agents
  • Consistent scoring criteria applied to every interaction
  • Low-confidence cases flagged for human review, never force-scored
