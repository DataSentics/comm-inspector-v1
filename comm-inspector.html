What it detects

The signals hidden in everyday conversations

Comm Inspector listens to what clients actually say and turns it into structured, CRM-ready signals — from a single call, chat, or email.

  • ✓ Life events — a home purchase, a new car, a move, or a change in the family
  • ✓ Financial needs and buying intent expressed in conversation
  • ✓ Voice of customer — needs, pain points, and product & service feedback
  • ✓ Cross-sell and up-sell opportunities surfaced from routine calls
  • ✓ Sentiment and engagement per interaction
  • ✓ Product and campaign mentions worth following up
Where the insights go

Straight into the tools your teams already use

Enriched client profiles

Detected needs, intent, and life events flow into your CRM, enriching each client profile automatically.

Campaign-ready segments

Signals are pushed to your marketing-automation tools, so campaigns target real, expressed needs — not guesses.

One consistent taxonomy

Every signal uses the same taxonomy across teams, so insights stay comparable across products and over time.
