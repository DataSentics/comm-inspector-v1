Worked example

From one call to business value

Example call transcript
Agent

Good day, Nováková speaking. How can I help you?

Client

Hello, this is Petr. I'm calling because when I travel to South America, to Chile, my card payments sometimes get blocked — I wanted to let you know in advance.

Agent

Thank you for telling us in advance. And how long will you be there, from when to when?

Client

I'll be there from the 17th until the 27th.

Agent

Noted — I'll record this so our security team won't block your payments. Have a nice trip, goodbye.

AI analysis
AI analysis by Comm Inspector Speech-to-text · LLM analysis · Insight detection
  • Travel notification Client travelling to South America (Chile)
  • Travel dates 17th – 27th (10 days)
  • Reason for call Prevent account monitoring blocking payments
  • Client Petr
  • Interaction outcome Travel note recorded in the system
