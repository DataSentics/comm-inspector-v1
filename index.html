How it works

From customer conversations to actionable insights

AI Communication Inspector captures the full context of each interaction and answers the business questions behind every conversation.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Calls, meetings, chats, emails and notes — as audio files or existing text.

  3. 02

    Process

    Audio is transcribed and all communication data is prepared for consistent AI analysis.

  5. 03

    Inspect

    AI evaluates every interaction against your predefined business, sales, customer experience or compliance questions.

  7. 04

    Deliver

    Insights are delivered as structured data, dashboards and signals for CRM, campaigns, reporting and customer profile enrichment.

CASE STUDY — MAJOR BANKING GROUP, EU

The challenge

Retail contact-centre calls contained real commercial signal — clients mentioning life events, financial needs, and product interest in passing — but this was invisible to sales and marketing, and volumes were too large to review by hand.

The solution

AI Communication Inspector analysed customer calls against a taxonomy of sales and engagement signals, converting them into structured, CRM-ready insight records delivered automatically into the bank's marketing-automation tooling.

The results

Campaigns built on the AI-generated insights achieved above-average conversion rates compared to the bank's other campaign sources.

SECONDARY REFERENCE — FINANCIAL SERVICES, PHILIPPINES (30M+ CALLS/YEAR)

Automated call supervision and quality control: every call evaluated against multiple predefined QA conditions, with violation flags, the reasoning behind each flag, and a per-call score on a dashboard. Public reference — a live reference call is available on request.
